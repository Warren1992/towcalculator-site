Privacy Policy

TowCalculator.net is built to be useful without collecting personal information. This page explains what may be collected automatically (like basic site analytics and advertising cookies) and what that means for you.

What we collect

TowCalculator.net does not require an account, login, or form submission to use the calculator or read posts.

Like most websites, basic non-personal information may be collected automatically, such as:

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites function and (when ads are used) help display relevant advertising.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features of websites in general may work less reliably when cookies are disabled.
