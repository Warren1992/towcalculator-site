Can a Tesla Model Y Tow a Travel Trailer?

Check the Tesla U.S. Model Y towing manual for your exact vehicle before selecting a trailer. Loaded trailer weight, measured tongue weight and vehicle load must each be within their own applicable limits.

Worked example: 3,500 lb loaded trailer

Do not tow this setup. The example puts 420 lb on the hitch, which is 70 lb over the 350 lb limit shown for the highest-rated configuration in the linked Tesla manual. The 350 lb figure applies to configurations that Tesla rates for 3,500 lb. The manual lists lower limits for some combinations of wheels, seating and passengers. Verify your vehicle’s exact configuration before using either number.

The estimated 995 lb of vehicle load includes the driver, passenger, vehicle cargo, added hitch hardware and tongue weight. Having payload left does not make an over-limit tongue weight acceptable.

Compare the actual loaded trailer weight and measured tongue weight against the limits for both the vehicle and the trailer. Also verify vehicle and axle load limits and brake requirements. If the permitted loading ranges do not overlap, choose a different trailer or tow vehicle. Do not shift cargo rearward just to hit a lower tongue-weight number.

Check actual weights before towing

The calculator can help screen a proposed setup, but it cannot certify it. Follow the vehicle and trailer manufacturers’ loading instructions and verify the loaded setup with scales. Plan for shorter driving range while towing.
