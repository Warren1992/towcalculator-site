About TowCalculator.net

TowCalculator.net was built to answer a simple question:

“Can this vehicle tow that trailer safely in the real world?”

Most towing discussions focus on advertised tow ratings. In practice, many towing setups fail long before reaching that number — usually due to payload limits, tongue weight, passengers, cargo, or hitch setup.

Payload-First Towing

TowCalculator.net focuses on the numbers that actually stop people from towing safely:

  • Payload capacity
  • Tongue weight
  • Passenger and cargo weight
  • Hitch and receiver limits

This site exists to bridge the gap between brochure numbers and real-world setups.

Who This Site Is For

TowCalculator.net is designed for:

  • First-time travel trailer owners
  • People upgrading trailers or vehicles
  • Anyone confused by conflicting tow ratings
  • Drivers who want clear, honest answers

No Brand Bias

TowCalculator.net is not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer. All examples are based on publicly available specifications and real-world towing principles.

The goal is not to sell vehicles or trailers — it’s to help people avoid unsafe or unrealistic towing setups.
